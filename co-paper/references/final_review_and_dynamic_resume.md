# Co-Completer Finalization and Resume

After `manuscript/full_manuscript.md`, hand off to `$co-completer` for rigor, novelty, evidence sufficiency, source-mode, missing-experiment, and readiness review.

Required outputs:

- `manuscript/completion_review.md`
- `manuscript/completion_revision_plan.md`
- `manuscript/completion_decision_menu.md`
- marked manuscript files when revisions are performed

If a major claim uses assumed or virtual results, judge the manuscript not ready for real-evidence submission.

## Route menu

1. `complete_project`: save `project_completion_record.md`.
2. `dynamic_branch`: resume a prior plan, module, result, figure, claim, or manuscript checkpoint under `branches/YYYYMMDD_<target>/`.
3. `new_evidence_cycle`: start a revised `co-plan -> co-result` cycle.

Preserve prior outputs and ledgers. Do not automatically call Co-Search, Co-Mimic, Co-Topic, or Co-Debate during resume unless the user explicitly switches back to topic development.
