# Co-Mimic Routine and Handoff Schema

## One-sentence routine

In `[context]`, use `[bioinformatics exploration]` to identify `[phenotype/candidate/???]`, test it through `[experimental perturbation, mechanism, direct interaction, rescue]`, validate reproducibility through `[independent bioinformatics validation]`, and connect the evidence to `[translational endpoint]`.

## Routine report

1. Entry problem.
2. Bioinformatics exploration job.
3. Candidate prioritization.
4. Experimental validation ladder.
5. Rescue or causal test.
6. Bioinformatics validation job.
7. Figure sequence.
8. Translational endpoint.
9. What can be copied structurally.
10. What must not be copied biologically.

## Figure logic columns

`figure,panel,panel_job,input_data_or_assay,comparison,claim_supported,evidence_module,evidence_tier,reusable_pattern,provenance,notes`

## Unknown-node handoff

`slot_id,placeholder,biological_gap,known_targets_to_exclude,allowed_family_hints,discovery_route,experimental_test,bioinformatics_validation_route,minimum_novelty_filter`

## Co-Topic handoff

Include the distilled routine, allowed biological substitutions, fixed constraints, `???` slots, exclusions, reusable figure jobs, three-module evidence expectations, reviewer risks, and unresolved uncertainties. Do not generate final topic candidates in Co-Mimic.
